# Step 4B: Article-wise Checked Quantities

## What is Step 4B?

**Step 4B** is a quality control system in the Checking Floor that categorizes checked items into four quality categories before they move to the next production floor.

## The Four Quality Categories

### 🟢 M1 - Good Quality
- **What it means**: Items that pass all quality checks
- **What happens**: Auto-transfer to Washing floor
- **Color**: Green

### 🟡 M2 - Needs Repair  
- **What it means**: Items requiring repair or review
- **What happens**: Supervisor must review and decide
- **Color**: Yellow
- **Special**: Has a repair review sub-step

### 🟠 M3 - Minor Defects
- **What it means**: Items with minor defects that can be fixed
- **What happens**: Can be corrected and moved forward
- **Color**: Orange

### 🔴 M4 - Major Defects
- **What it means**: Items with major defects requiring significant repair
- **What happens**: Extensive rework or rejection
- **Color**: Red

## Step 4B.1: M2 Repair Review

When M2 items exist, supervisors must review them:

### Repair Status Options
1. **Not Required**: No repair needed
2. **In Review**: Currently being reviewed  
3. **Repaired**: Repair completed successfully
4. **Rejected**: Item cannot be repaired

### M2 Item Shifting
Supervisors can move M2 items to other categories:
- **M2 → M1**: Item repaired and now good quality
- **M2 → M3**: Item has minor defects after review
- **M2 → M4**: Item has major defects after review

**How it works**: Click buttons to shift 10 items at a time from M2 to other categories.
